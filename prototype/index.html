FlexiStock is a point-of-sale and inventory app you can set up your way. Turn on only the features your store needs.

Features

Fast sales

Ring up items, take cash or e-wallet payments, and print a receipt.

Stock you can trust

Every item in or out is recorded, with batch numbers and expiry dates when you need them.

Credit (utang)

Sell on credit with a due date and track what each customer still owes.

Turn features on or off

A hardware store does not need expiry tracking. Switch off what you do not use.

Staff roles

Choose exactly what each staff member can do.

Daily numbers

See today's sales, profit, and low-stock items at a glance.

How it works

1. Download

Install FlexiStock on your store computer.

2. Set up your store

Add your products and choose your features.

3. Start selling

Ring up sales and watch your stock update.
